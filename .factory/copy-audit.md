## Labels and headings

Job headline: **Hear selected image text aloud**.
Primary action: **Try it with sample data**.
Section headings name their content: **Read a screenshot or image**, **How to read text aloud**, **What stays on your device**, and **Export or import your reader data**.
All buttons start with a result-oriented verb: Try, Choose, Load, Clear, Reset, Read, Repeat, Speak, Stop, Export, Import, Reload, or Go.
